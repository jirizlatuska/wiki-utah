The Route at a Glance

A clockwise loop of ~2,000 miles from Phoenix covering the Colorado Plateau's most remote and spectacular terrain — 6 driving legs, 14–18 days on the road.

LEG 1 Phoenix → Tuba City  ·  Apache Trail, Roosevelt Dam, Meteor Crater, Coal Mine Canyon
LEG 2 Tuba City → Stateline  ·  Navajo Bridge, Bryce Canyon, Kodachrome Basin
LEG 3 Stateline → Goblin Valley  ·  Coyote Buttes South, Alstrom Point, Escalante
LEG 4 Goblin Valley → Mt. Pennell  ·  Cathedral Valley, Bentonite Hills, Henry Mtns
LEG 5 Hanksville → Muley Point  ·  White Rim Road, Natural Bridges, Cedar Mesa
LEG 6 Muley Point → Phoenix  ·  Monument Valley, Grand Canyon, Sedona, Verde Canyon Railroad
⚠ Advance Permits Required Three stops have strict daily quotas — book early via Recreation.gov: White Pocket (Vermilion Cliffs) · Coyote Buttes South · White Rim Road (Canyonlands)

All route data from GPS KMZ files. Detailed wiki pages at wiki/index.md.

What this trip is

A long loop through the wildest, least-visited corners of the American Southwest — deep canyons, secret slickrock formations, free-roaming bison, ancient footprints, and the bluest night skies on earth.

Not your typical road trip

  • Permit-only landscapes most people never see
  • Wild bison roaming freely around the car
  • Condors with 9-foot wingspans at eye level
  • A valley of alien mushroom rocks you can climb
  • Real dinosaur footprints — guided, free, and unforgettable
  • A canyon you can only reach by steam train

Best for: Families with children, photographers, 4WD enthusiasts, anyone who wants to feel genuinely small.

Legs 1 – 3  ·  Phoenix to Escalante
Leg 1 — PHX to Tuba City Day 1–2
▸Apache Trail (AZ-88) — narrow unpaved switchback road above Canyon Lake, finishing at Roosevelt Dam
▸Mogollon Rim — dramatic escarpment where the Sonoran desert meets the Colorado Plateau
▸Meteor Crater — 50,000-year-old impact site; museum and rim walk
▸Coal Mine Canyon — hidden Navajo canyon; rim camping
▸Moenave Dinosaur Tracks ★ — Navajo-guided; 200+ footprints in living rock
Leg 2 — Tuba City to Stateline Day 3–6
▸Navajo Bridge — condors roosting at eye level; 467 ft above the Colorado
▸White Pocket ★ (permit) — swirling, candy-striped Navajo Sandstone; one of a kind
▸Bryce Canyon — world's largest hoodoo amphitheater; prairie dogs; junior rangers
▸Kodachrome Basin — sedimentary spires; Grosvenor Arch double arch
▸Cottonwood Canyon Road through Grand Staircase
Leg 3 — Stateline to Goblin Valley Day 7–9
▸Coyote Buttes South ★ (permit) — Paw Hole slickrock bowl; 10 visitors/day
▸Alstrom Point — Lake Powell panorama from the Kaiparowits rim
▸Smokey Mountain Road — remote dirt crossing of the Kaiparowits Plateau
▸Escalante — slot canyons, petrified forest
▸Upper Muley Twist Canyon — Capitol Reef backcountry
▸Goblin Valley ★ — hundreds of climbable mushroom rocks; kids roam free

★ = top pick  ·  (permit) = advance booking required via Recreation.gov

Legs 4 – 6  ·  Henry Mountains to Phoenix
Leg 4 — Goblin Valley to Mt. Pennell Day 10–11
▸Moonscape Overlook — San Rafael Swell badlands panorama
▸Cathedral Valley — monolithic sandstone temples in Capitol Reef's remote north
▸Bentonite Hills — technicolor clay badlands (impassable when wet)
▸Henry Mountains — Wild Bison ★ — 300–400 bison roaming freely; last unmapped range in the lower 48
Leg 5 — Hanksville to Muley Point Day 12–14
▸White Rim Road ★ (permit) — 100 mi 4WD loop around Canyonlands; Shafer Trail descent, White Crack camp
▸Mineral Bottom Switchbacks — dramatic exit to the Green River
▸Natural Bridges NM — three bridges; America's first Dark Sky Park; Milky Way overhead
▸Muley Point ★ — Cedar Mesa rim; 1,000 ft drop; Monument Valley on the horizon
Leg 6 — Muley Point to Phoenix Day 15–17
▸Valley of the Gods — free 17-mile BLM dirt loop through sandstone buttes
▸Monument Valley ★ — the West Mitten and East Mitten at sunrise
▸Grand Canyon — Desert View Watchtower on the South Rim
▸Sedona — red-rock canyon hiking
▸Verde Canyon Railroad ★ — 4-hr canyon train; herons, eagles, 680 ft tunnel
▸Organ Pipe Cactus NM · Kitt Peak Observatory
