# Meteor Crater Area

**Summary**: The corridor from Roosevelt Lake northeast to Winslow/Holbrook, covering Homolovi ruins, Little Painted Desert, Petrified Forest, and Meteor Crater — plus Coal Mine Canyon and Tuba City dinosaur tracks slightly further north.

**Sources**: User planning notes.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-18

---

## Getting There

From Roosevelt Lake: US-60 north through Globe, then AZ-260 or AZ-77 to Show Low / Holbrook / Winslow area. Alternatively via Salt River Canyon (US-60) — dramatic gorge drive worth the time.

---

## Points of Interest

### Salt River Canyon (en route)
- US-60 drops into a deep canyon before ascending to the White Mountains plateau
- Good pullouts; geology visible from the road

### Homolovi Ruins State Park — Winslow
- 300+ Ancestral Puebloan sites (Hopi ancestors, ~1330–1400 AD)
- Homolovi II open to self-guided visits: ~1,200 rooms, interpretive signage, accessible trail
- Good for kids — visible ruins without a long hike
- 1 mile north of Winslow off I-40

### Little Painted Desert County Park — near Winslow
- Overlook over colorful badlands geology; short stop
- Already in user's saved Google Maps places (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

### Meteor Crater Natural Landmark
- Best-preserved meteorite impact crater on Earth; 550 ft deep, ~3/4 mile wide
- Discovery Center: hands-on exhibits, Apollo 11 capsule, 4D theater — very kid-friendly
- 35 miles east of Flagstaff on I-40 (exit 233)

### Petrified Forest National Park — Holbrook
- World's largest concentration of petrified wood; 225 million years old
- Also contains: Painted Desert overlooks, Puerco Pueblo petroglyphs (~650 AD), Newspaper Rock (park version)
- Drive-through park (~28 miles one way); no camping inside the park
- ~1 hour east of Winslow on I-40

---

## Dinosaur Tracks — Tuba City (Navajo Nation)
- **Tuba City Dinosaur Tracks** (Moenave Formation): ~200 Early Jurassic footprints (~200 million years old), preserved in ancient riverbed
- Location: 6 miles west of Tuba City on US-160, turn onto Route 23
- Navajo guides available on-site (tips $5–20); no formal admission fee
- Accessible and very good for a child
- **Note**: on Navajo Nation land — check current access requirements (Navajo Parks & Recreation)
- Slight detour north of Meteor Crater; pairs well with Coal Mine Canyon

---

## Coal Mine Canyon (aka Coal Canyon) — near Tuba City
- Deeply eroded canyon with striking red, white, and black striped cliffs
- Historic Navajo/Hopi coal mine operated 1908–1950
- Located ~15 miles southeast of Tuba City
- **Access**: rim is Navajo Nation; interior is Hopi Nation — check current permit requirements before visiting
- Pairs well with the Tuba City dinosaur tracks (same day loop)

---

## Volcanic Remains — Flagstaff Area (slight detour)
If routing via Flagstaff instead of Holbrook:

### Sunset Crater Volcano National Monument
- Cinder cone from eruption ~1085 AD; lava flow fields, volcanic landscape
- 15 miles north of Flagstaff on US-89

### Wupatki National Monument
- Ancestral Puebloan ruins built on volcanic landscape shortly after Sunset Crater eruption
- Loop road connects both monuments; half-day trip from Flagstaff

---

## Camping

No camping inside Petrified Forest NP. Options nearby:
- **Homolovi State Park** has a campground (hookups available but not needed; reservable)
- **Coconino National Forest** dispersed camping near Flagstaff if routing that way
- Private campgrounds in Holbrook/Winslow (functional, nothing scenic)

---

## Animal Sites (Child-Friendly)
- **Bearizona Wildlife Park** (Williams, AZ): drive-through and walk-through with bears, wolves, bison, mountain lions in natural habitat enclosures — excellent for kids; on the way if routing via I-40/Williams
