## Dates

**July 23 – August 16** (25 days)

### Season notes

- Peak summer — temperatures in desert areas (Tucson, Moab, Page) regularly exceed 100°F / 38°C
- **Monsoon season**: mid-July through September brings afternoon thunderstorms across Arizona and southern Utah — flash flood risk in slot canyons is significant; always check forecasts before entering narrow canyons (Buckskin Gulch, Zebra Slot, Spooky, Willis Creek, etc.)
- Higher elevations (Bryce, Cedar Breaks, Flagstaff) will be cooler and more comfortable
- Saguaro NP in late July: extreme heat; plan hikes for early morning only

- SUV, tent camping — no trailer
- Relevant for campsite selection (no hookups needed) and road access (4WD/high-clearance tracks viable)

## Leg 1 — Saguaro National Park

- **Drive**: PHX → Tucson (~2 hrs)
- **Camp**: Gilbert Ray Campground, adjacent to Saguaro NP West (Tucson Mountain District) — one night, tent + SUV
- See [[saguaro-national-park]] for details and reservation notes

---

## Leg 2 — Apache Trail → Meteor Crater area

- **Dates**: ~July 24–27 (rough, TBD)
- **Drive**: Tucson → Apache Trail (AZ-88) → Roosevelt Lake → US-60 north → Winslow/Meteor Crater area
- **Camp**: Tonto National Forest sites along Apache Trail / Roosevelt Lake; see [[apache-trail]]
- **Key stops**: Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum (Tucson), Apache Trail viewpoints, Tonto National Monument, Homolovi Ruins, Little Painted Desert, Meteor Crater
- **Geology/history emphasis**: volcanic, prehistoric, petroglyph sites along the way
- See [[apache-trail]] and [[meteor-crater-area]] for details

---

## Leg 3 — Monument Valley → Goosenecks → Page

- **Dates**: ~July 26–27
- **Drive**: Meteor Crater area → US-160 east → Monument Valley → US-163 north → Goosenecks → US-89 west → Page
- **Camp**: The View Campground (Monument Valley) or Lone Rock Beach (Page)
- **Key stops**: Monument Valley drive loop, Goosenecks overlook, Horseshoe Bend, Antelope Canyon (book tours in advance — July sells out weeks ahead)
- See [[monument-valley-goosenecks]] and [[page-area]]

---

## Leg 4 — Kanab / Cottonwood Canyon / Kodachrome

- **Dates**: ~July 28–29
- **Drive**: Page → US-89 west → Kanab → Cottonwood Canyon Road → Kodachrome Basin → back to US-89 → House Rock Valley Road
- **Camp**: Coral Pink Sand Dunes SP or Kodachrome Basin SP or dispersed near Stateline
- **Key stops**: Coral Pink Sand Dunes, Cottonwood Canyon Road (weather permitting), Grosvenor Arch, Kodachrome Basin
- **Note**: Cottonwood Canyon Road is clay — impassable when wet. Have a fallback plan if it rained.
- This leg may need to be trimmed; see [[kanab-area]] for time budget options

---

## Leg 5 — Coyote Buttes

- **Dates**: July 30 – August 1
- **Camp**: Stateline Campground (BLM, House Rock Valley Road)
- July 30: **Coyote Buttes South** (permit confirmed)
- July 31 & Aug 1: **The Wave / Coyote Buttes North** (lottery pending)
- See [[coyote-buttes]]

---

## Leg 6 — Goblin Valley

- **Dates**: ~August 3–4
- **Drive**: Stateline → US-89 north → UT-12 or US-89/UT-9 → UT-24 → Goblin Valley (~3.5 hrs)
- **Camp**: Goblin Valley State Park campground (reserve in advance) or nearby BLM
- **Key stops**: Valley of Goblins, Little Wild Horse Canyon, Wedge Overlook
- See [[goblin-valley]]
