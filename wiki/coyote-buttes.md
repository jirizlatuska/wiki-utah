## Stateline Campground

- BLM dispersed camping at the Utah/Arizona border near House Rock Valley Road (BLM-1065)
- No facilities, no fee; first come, first served
- Closest camp to both trailheads
- GPS: ~37.00°N, 112.00°W (verify exact coordinates before arriving)
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip via Wire Pass / Coyote Buttes area)

## Coyote Buttes South (July 30)

- Access: BLM House Rock Valley Road south of Stateline, then hiking
- Less visited than The Wave; equally striking sandstone formations — The Cottonwood Cove, The Dungeons, South Coyote Buttes teepees
- No established trail; navigation by map/GPS required
- Allow full day

---

## Coyote Buttes North / The Wave (Jul 31 & Aug 1)

- Access: Wire Pass Trailhead, off House Rock Valley Road
- 6-mile round trip; no trail; cross-country navigation on slickrock
- The Wave is a Jurassic-era Navajo Sandstone formation; among the most photogenic spots in the Southwest
- Also in the area: Top Rock Arch, Second Wave, Melody Arch
- Start early — July heat on open slickrock is extreme by mid-morning

---

## House Rock Valley Road Conditions

- BLM-1065: unpaved, passable for SUV when dry
- After rain: clay sections become slick — same caution as Cottonwood Canyon Road
- Check BLM Arizona Strip conditions before driving in
