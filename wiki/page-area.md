# Page Area

**Summary**: Page, AZ and surroundings — Lake Powell, Horseshoe Bend, Antelope Canyon, and nearby Glen Canyon.

**Sources**: User planning notes.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-18

---

## Horseshoe Bend

- Iconic entrenched meander of the Colorado River; 270° bend visible from a single overlook
- Short walk from parking lot (~1.5 miles round trip, flat)
- Very crowded in summer; go early morning or at sunset
- No fee to enter the overlook area (Glen Canyon NRA)

---

## Antelope Canyon

Two separate slot canyons, both on Navajo Nation land near Page — guided tours only.

| Canyon | Type | Character |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Upper Antelope (Tsé bighánílíní) | Walk-through | Famous light beams; crowded; paved floor |
| Lower Antelope (Hazdistazí) | Ladder/descent | Less crowded; more intimate; slightly longer |

**Book in advance**: July is peak season — tours sell out weeks ahead. Book as soon as dates are confirmed.
- Operators: Antelope Canyon Navajo Tours, Ken's Tours, and others
- Cost: ~$60–90/person for standard tours (needs verification)
- Light beams in Upper Antelope: best around midday, April–September

## Lake Powell / Wahweap Area

- Glen Canyon NRA; Wahweap Marina is the main hub near Page
- Boat tours, kayak rentals available
- **Lone Rock Beach**: dispersed camping on the sand at the lake edge — accessible by SUV; fun for families; very basic (no shade, no water)
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (Lone Rock, Wahweap — source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

---

## Camping Near Page

- **Lone Rock Beach** (BLM/NPS): best option for a family — drive onto the beach, tent on sand; $14/night; no reservations
- Page has commercial campgrounds (functional but not scenic)

---

## Routing Context

- Monument Valley → Page: ~1.5 hrs via US-160 west to US-89 north
- Page → Kanab: ~1.25 hrs via US-89 west
- Page → Stateline Campground (House Rock): ~1.5 hrs via US-89 south to House Rock Valley Road
