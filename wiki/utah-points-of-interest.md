## Notes on Willis Creek

> "Great slot canyon! Do not navigate via Adams Wash — it's very dangerous. Stay on BLM500 / Skutumpah Rd instead. Google Maps navigated us via Adams Wash. It was very treacherous and would not advise it even with a 4WD high-clearance SUV. Took 50 minutes to make 4.7 miles. The slot canyon itself is very pretty and easy to access."

(source: Willis Creek Slot Canyon trailhead entry, Want to go.csv)
