# Kanab Area

**Summary**: Kanab, UT and surrounding attractions — Coral Pink Sand Dunes, Cottonwood Canyon Road, Grosvenor Arch, and Kodachrome Basin State Park.

## Overview

Kanab sits at the crossroads of several major routes and is a natural base for the Grand Staircase-Escalante region. From here, Cottonwood Canyon Road heads northeast through some of the most remote and scenic terrain in the state.

---

## Coral Pink Sand Dunes State Park

- ~12 miles northwest of Kanab off US-89
- Unusual pink-tinted sand dunes in a forested setting; good for kids
- State park campground on site (reservable)
- Allow 1–2 hours for a visit

---

## Cottonwood Canyon Road (BLM-700)

- ~46 miles of unpaved road connecting US-89 (south, near Kanab) to UT-12 (north, near Cannonville)
- **Road condition**: clay surface — can become completely impassable when wet; given July monsoon season, check conditions before committing. After rain, wait 24–48 hours minimum.
- Scenery: Cockscomb formation, red-rock canyon walls, remote and largely empty
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

### Grosvenor Arch
- Double arch — one of the most striking arches in the region
- Short walk from the road; signed turnoff ~30 miles north of US-89
- Worth the detour even if not doing the full road

### Kodachrome Basin State Park
- Near Cannonville, at the north end of Cottonwood Canyon Road
- Unique sedimentary spires (sand pipes) and colorful geology
- State park campground available (reservable); good facilities
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

## Routing Note

Doing the full Cottonwood Canyon Road (US-89 → Kodachrome → Cannonville) is a one-way ~46-mile drive; you either continue north toward Bryce/Escalante, or turn back. To get from Kodachrome to Stateline Campground (Coyote Buttes), the fastest route is back south on Cottonwood Canyon Road to US-89, then US-89 east to House Rock Valley Road — roughly 2.5 hours, road conditions permitting.

## Time Budget Reality Check

Monument Valley + Goosenecks + Page + Kanab + Coral Pink + full Cottonwood Canyon Road + Kodachrome before July 30 is tight. Rough estimate from Meteor Crater area:

| Day | Option A (full) | Option B (trimmed) |
|-----|----------------|-------------------|
| ~Jul 26 | Monument Valley | Monument Valley |
| ~Jul 27 | Goosenecks → Page | Goosenecks → Page |
| ~Jul 28 | Kanab + Coral Pink + Cottonwood Rd | Kanab + Coral Pink only |
| ~Jul 29 | Kodachrome → Stateline | Stateline (positioned for Jul 30) |
| Jul 30 | **Coyote Buttes South (permit)** | **Coyote Buttes South (permit)** |

Option A requires no delays and dry roads on Cottonwood Canyon. Option B is more realistic given monsoon uncertainty.

## Animal Note

**Best Friends Animal Sanctuary** is located just outside Kanab — the largest no-kill animal sanctuary in the US; tours available, kid-friendly.
