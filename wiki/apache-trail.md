# Apache Trail

**Summary**: Scenic byway (AZ-88) east of Phoenix through the Superstition Mountains, passing chain of desert lakes to Roosevelt Dam. First major driving leg after Saguaro NP.

**Sources**: User planning notes.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-18

---

## Overview

Apache Trail (AZ-88) runs ~40 miles from Apache Junction to Roosevelt Dam. The western paved section is dramatic enough; east of Tortilla Flat the road turns unpaved, narrow, and cliff-hugging — fine for an SUV, slow going. Plan 3–4 hours for the full drive with stops.

---

## Stops Along the Trail (west to east)

### Goldfield Ghost Town
- Historic mining town, reconstructed; touristy but kid-friendly
- Just outside Apache Junction at the trailhead

### Canyon Lake Vista
- Blue lake framed by red canyon walls; good short stop

### Tortilla Flat
- Small historic settlement; last services before the unpaved section
- Good place to fuel up on water/snacks

### Fish Creek Hill Overlook
- Best viewpoint on the trail — dramatic drop into Fish Creek Canyon
- Short walk from pullout

### Apache Lake Vista
- Painted Cliffs views over the lake

### Tonto National Monument
- Cliff dwellings built by the Salado people (~1300s AD), above Roosevelt Lake
- Lower Ruin: self-guided, 1-mile round trip; manageable for a child
- Upper Ruin: ranger-guided only, longer hike — book in advance
- (source: User planning notes; details need verification)

### Roosevelt Dam Overlook
- Engineering views; the dam impounds Roosevelt Lake

## Camping — Tonto National Forest

All sites are within Tonto National Forest; no hookups needed.

| Campground | Location | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Tortilla Campground** | Canyon Lake | Along AZ-88; basic |
| **Burnt Corral Campground** | Apache Lake | 76 sites on shoreline; vault toilets, water, boat launch |
| **Cholla Campground** | Roosevelt Lake | 206 sites; shade ramadas, playground, showers — best for families |

**Recommendation**: Cholla Campground at Roosevelt Lake is the most comfortable option for a family group.

## Animal / Nature Stop at Start of Leg

### Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum (Tucson)
- Zoo, botanical garden, and natural history museum in one — located right next to Gilbert Ray Campground / Saguaro NP West
- Excellent for a child; live desert animals (javelinas, raptors, mountain lions, otters, hummingbirds)
- Open early; visit in the morning before driving Apache Trail

## Notes

- Unpaved section (Tortilla Flat → Roosevelt) is passable in an SUV; dusty, slow — allow extra time
- No gas stations east of Tortilla Flat until Globe
- July heat: start driving early; Fish Creek area has no shade
