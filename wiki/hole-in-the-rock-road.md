# Hole in the Rock Road & Escalante Slot Canyons

**Summary**: BLM road south from Escalante toward Lake Powell, with access to the most accessible slot canyons in Grand Staircase-Escalante: Peek-a-Boo, Spooky, Zebra, and others.

**Sources**: User planning notes; takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip

**Last updated**: 2026-05-18

---

## Hole in the Rock Road (BLM-200)

- Departs Escalante south; ~57 miles to the historic Hole-in-the-Rock crossing at Lake Powell
- Unpaved from mile 5 onward; road quality degrades past Devil's Garden
- Last ~10 miles are very rough — manageable in SUV in dry conditions but slow
- Road becomes impassable when wet (clay sections); monsoon risk applies

### Key Stops by Mile

| Mile | Stop | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| ~5 | Moqui Cave | Privately owned tourist cave; dinosaur tracks display; admission fee; kid-friendly |
| ~12 | Devil's Garden | BLM hoodoos and arches; short easy walk; excellent for a child; free |
| ~26 | Dry Fork Road turnoff | Access to Peek-a-Boo, Spooky, Dry Fork Narrows |
| ~37 | Dance Hall Rock | Large sandstone boulder used for dances during 1879 pioneer trek; historical marker |
| ~57 | Hole-in-the-Rock | Historic blasted slot where pioneers lowered wagons to the Colorado River; viewpoint over Lake Powell |

## Slot Canyons (off Dry Fork Road, ~mile 26)

All of these are accessible from the Dry Fork trailhead. Morning start is essential — flash flood risk in confined canyons.

### Peek-a-Boo Slot Canyon
- ~3 miles round trip from Dry Fork trailhead
- Narrow, sculpted Navajo Sandstone; some short climbs/squeezes
- Accessible for a child with some assistance
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

### Spooky Gulch
- ~4 miles round trip; can be combined with Peek-a-Boo as a loop
- Extremely narrow — the tightest canyon on the road; some passages require removing packs
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

### Dry Fork Narrows
- Between Peek-a-Boo and Spooky; often combined into a full loop
- Less technical; good warmup or standalone

### Zebra Slot Canyon
- Separate trailhead ~0.5 miles before Dry Fork turnoff
- ~5.5 miles round trip
- Distinctive zebra-striped sandstone walls
- Some scrambling; flash flood terrain — morning only
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

## Combined Day Option

A full Peek-a-Boo + Spooky + Zebra day is possible but long (~12+ miles total hiking plus the drive). Prioritize based on conditions and energy:
- **Quickest payoff**: Peek-a-Boo + Spooky loop (~4–5 miles, most iconic)
- **If extra time**: add Zebra Canyon (separate trailhead, separate start)
- **Child-friendly**: Peek-a-Boo is the most manageable; Spooky gets very tight

---

## Camping Near Escalante

- **Calf Creek Campground** (BLM, UT-12): best scenic option; no reservations
- **Escalante Petrified Forest State Park**: reliable fallback; reservable
- Dispersed BLM camping along Hole-in-the-Rock Road (before Devil's Garden, flat pullouts)

---

## Routing Note

If coming from Capitol Reef / Muley Twist via Burr Trail → Boulder, this is a natural continuation: Boulder → UT-12 west 26 miles → Escalante → Hole-in-the-Rock Road south. No backtracking required.
