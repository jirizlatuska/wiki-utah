# Saguaro National Park

**Summary**: National park flanking Tucson, AZ, protecting the iconic saguaro cactus. Split into two separate districts on opposite sides of the city.

**Sources**: User planning notes.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-18

---

## Overview

Saguaro NP is divided into two districts, both within easy reach of Tucson:

| District | Side | Character |
|----------|------|-----------|
| Rincon Mountain District (East) | East of Tucson | Larger; backcountry camping; higher elevation forest |
| Tucson Mountain District (West) | West of Tucson | More iconic saguaro density; closer to Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum |

---

## Camping

**Planned**: Gilbert Ray Campground — Pima County park adjacent to the Tucson Mountain District (West).

- Tent camping, SUV (no trailer)
- Reservable at the Pima County parks reservation system (needs verification)
- Sites have picnic tables and fire rings; restrooms on site (needs verification)
- No hookups needed

Reserve in advance — popular site, especially October–April.

---

## Key Facts

- Nearest city: Tucson, AZ (~15 min from either district entrance)
- Drive from PHX: ~2 hours
- **Trip dates**: July 23–24 — peak summer; expect 100°F+ during the day; hike early morning only
- Entry fee: $25/vehicle (as of recent years — needs verification)
- No gas or food inside the park

---

## Things to Do

- Cactus Forest Loop Drive (East district, 8-mile paved loop)
- Signal Hill Petroglyphs (West district)
- Valley View Overlook Trail (West district)
- Rincon Mountain backcountry trails
