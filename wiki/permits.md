# Permits

**Summary**: Three stops on this trip require advance permits; all are booked through Recreation.gov and have strict daily quotas.

**Sources**: `raw/` KMZ route files (location identification); permit details need verification against current Recreation.gov listings.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-19.

---

## White Pocket

**Trip leg**: [[itinerary]] Leg 2
**Location**: Vermilion Cliffs National Monument, AZ
**Permit unit**: White Pocket Day Use

White Pocket is a surreal swirling sandstone formation in the Vermilion Cliffs. Access requires a high-clearance 4WD vehicle on deep sand. The site has a small daily visitor quota allocated by lottery.

- Booking: Recreation.gov — search "Vermilion Cliffs White Pocket" (needs verification)
- Lottery windows open several months in advance (needs verification)
- No camping on-site; nearest camping is Stateline Campground or BLM dispersed

See also: [[navajo-bridge]], [[cottonwood-canyon-road]]

## Coyote Buttes South (Paw Hole)

**Trip leg**: [[itinerary]] Leg 3
**Location**: Paria Canyon–Vermilion Cliffs Wilderness, AZ/UT border
**Permit unit**: Coyote Buttes South

Coyote Buttes South (CBS) is the less-visited complement to Coyote Buttes North (The Wave). Paw Hole is the main attraction: a bowl of crinkled, candy-striped Navajo Sandstone. Quota is roughly 10 permits per day (needs verification).

- Booking: Recreation.gov — "Coyote Buttes South" (needs verification)
- Daily lottery draws 4 months ahead and online day-before lottery
- Base camp at Stateline Campground (free BLM, ~10 sites)
- Trailhead at Wire Pass or House Rock Valley Road (needs verification)

See also: [[coyote-buttes-south]]

## White Rim Road

**Trip leg**: [[itinerary]] Leg 5
**Location**: Canyonlands National Park — Island in the Sky District, UT
**Permit unit**: White Rim Road Motorized

The White Rim Road is a 100-mile dirt loop around the Island in the Sky mesa at ~1,200 ft above the Colorado and Green rivers. Route on this trip enters via Shafer Trail (from Moab/US-191) and exits at Mineral Bottom. Allow 3 days minimum by 4WD.

Key waypoints on route (source: `BLM 0095 Hanksville to Muley Point.kmz`):
- Shafer Trail Viewpoint — top of the canyon rim descent
- White Crack — popular mid-trip campsite
- Mineral Bottom Switchbacks — exit via Green River side

- Booking: Recreation.gov — "White Rim Road" permit (needs verification)
- Permits required for overnight use; day trips also may require entry reservation (needs verification)
- No water along route; carry all water
- NPS entrance fee applies (America the Beautiful pass accepted)

See also: [[white-rim-road]], [[muley-point]]

## Booking strategy

Because two of the three permits (White Pocket and CBS) are lottery-based, the trip dates must be built around permit wins rather than the other way around. Recommended approach:

1. Enter lotteries for both White Pocket and CBS for the same general window
2. Once a permit is won, lock in surrounding nights and book White Rim Road for a compatible window
3. Non-permit legs are flexible and can be planned around permit dates
