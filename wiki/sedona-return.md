# Sedona & Return to Phoenix

## Overview

Sedona sits in Oak Creek Canyon at ~4,500 ft — cooler than Phoenix, surrounded by dramatic red sandstone formations. All main roads are paved; no risk of getting stuck in monsoon mud. A good decompression stop before the flight.

**Planned dates**: ~August 13–15 (2 nights)
**Departure**: PHX, August 16 (~2 hrs from Sedona)

## Other Key Stops

### Oak Creek Canyon / Slide Rock State Park
- Swimming holes in natural red rock slides along Oak Creek
- Extremely popular in summer — arrive early or expect queues
- Excellent for a child; one of the best hot-weather stops in Arizona

### Bell Rock
- Highly visible butte on the south end of Sedona; short loop trail; easy
- Good introduction to the landscape

### Boynton Canyon
- 6-mile round trip through a canyon with Sinagua cliff dwellings visible
- Cooler and shaded in the morning

### Devil's Bridge
- Most popular trail in Sedona (3.9 miles round trip); natural sandstone arch
- Requires booking a shuttle or arriving before 7am to park
- Worth it for the arch view; child-appropriate

---

## August Notes

- Sedona valley gets hot (95–105°F afternoon) but mornings are comfortable
- Monsoon storms roll through most afternoons — dramatic lightning over the red rocks; hike in the morning, watch the storm from camp in the afternoon
- All access roads are paved — no mud risk

---

## Camping / Lodging

- **Cave Springs Campground** (Coconino NF, Oak Creek Canyon): beautiful forest campground along the creek; reserve well in advance on recreation.gov — fills months ahead in summer
- **Manzanita Campground** (Coconino NF, Oak Creek Canyon): smaller; same situation
- Commercial campgrounds in the Village of Oak Creek (south Sedona) as fallback
- Note: if NF campgrounds are full, this may be a leg where a motel/cabin makes sense — a comfortable end to the trip

---

## Drive to PHX (Aug 16)

- Sedona → Phoenix: ~2 hours via AZ-179 south to I-17 south
- OR scenic: US-89A through Jerome and Prescott (~3 hours, very scenic)
- Allow extra time for airport return: PHX is a large airport; 2–3 hours before international / 2 hours domestic recommended
