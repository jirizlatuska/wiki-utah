# White Rim Road — Canyonlands (Option B fork)

**Summary**: 100-mile 4WD route around the White Rim in Canyonlands Island in the Sky district. Two-day trip entering via Shafer Trail from Dead Horse Point area. Includes Black Crack.

**Sources**: User planning notes.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-18

---

## Overview

White Rim Road circles the White Rim sandstone bench ~1,200 ft below the Island in the Sky mesa and ~1,000 ft above the Colorado and Green Rivers. One of the premier 4WD routes in the Southwest — continuous canyon views, almost no other terrain like it.

- Total loop: ~100 miles
- Planned as 2 days (1 night camping on the road)
- Entry: **Shafer Trail** from near Dead Horse Point State Park
- Exit: Mineral Bottom Road (north) or return via Shafer

---

## Permit

**Permit confirmed** — overnight camping on White Rim Road.

- **Entry**: Shafer Trail
- **Camp**: White Crack Campsite
- **Exit**: Mineral Bottom Road

---

## Entry: Shafer Trail

- Steep, narrow switchbacks descending ~1,300 ft from the Island in the Sky rim to the White Rim bench
- Starts near the Shafer Canyon Overlook, a short drive from Dead Horse Point State Park
- One-way down only (too exposed for comfortable ascent)
- Fine for a capable SUV in dry conditions; very exposed on the switchbacks — not recommended in wet/muddy conditions

---

## Route Highlights (Clockwise from Shafer)

| Segment | Key Features |
|---------|-------------|
| Shafer Trail → Musselman Arch | Rim-edge driving; Musselman Arch (walkable natural arch on the slickrock) |
| Musselman → Airport Tower | Towers and spires; broad canyon views |
| Airport Tower → White Crack | Open bench driving; campsites near White Crack |
| **Black Crack** | Deep fissure in the White Rim sandstone — dramatic; short walk from road |
| White Crack → Hardscrabble | Green River side; quieter |
| Mineral Bottom area | Turnaround option or exit point |

## Camping Along the Road

Designated campsites only — each tied to the overnight permit. Sites are spread along the route; assigned at permit booking.

Notable sites:
- **White Crack Campsite**: on the rim edge; exceptional views; very popular (specify at booking)
- **Airport Campsite**: open exposure, big views
- Several others spaced for a 2-day itinerary

No water anywhere on the road — carry all water for 2 days.

---

## Dead Horse Point State Park (trailhead area)

- Spectacular overlook over entrenched Colorado River meanders
- State park campground available (reservable)
- Natural start/staging point the night before descending Shafer Trail
- Already in user's saved Google Maps (source: takeout-20260518T231412Z-3-001.zip)

## Routing to Sedona / Phoenix (return leg)

Moab/Canyonlands → US-191 south → Monticello → US-163 → Kayenta → US-160 → Flagstaff → Sedona → Phoenix
~7–8 hours driving; long but manageable as a two-segment day or with an overnight stop

---

## Notes

- August on the White Rim: very hot below the rim (90–105°F); start driving early
- After rain: the road is mostly rock and compacted gravel — more resilient than clay roads elsewhere, but Shafer Trail descent can be slick
- Cell coverage: essentially none on the entire route
