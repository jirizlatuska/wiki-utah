# Trasy z Google Maps (KMZ)

**Summary**: Přehled pěti KMZ souborů s trasami exportovanými z Google Maps, které dohromady pokrývají páteřní trasování cesty; body na trasách naplňují segmenty v [[casovy-rozvrh]].

**Sources**: 5× soubor Directions from ... .kmz ve složce raw/

**Last updated**: 2026-07-20

---

## 1. PHX → Moenave Dinosaur Tracks (segment 1)

PHX → Gilbert Ray Campground → Apache Junction → Apache Trail (AZ-88) → Roosevelt Dam → Mogollon Rim → Meteor Crater → Coal Mine Canyon (rim camping) → Moenave Dinosaur Tracks (source: Directions from PHX to Moenave Dinosaur Tracks.kmz).

⚠️ Úsek Apache Trail je neprůjezdný — viz [[apache-trail-closure]]; náhradní trasování viz [[segment-1-revised-plan]].

## 2. Moenave Dinosaur Tracks → Stateline Campground (segment 2)

Moenave → Navajo Bridge (US-89A) → White Pocket → Kanab → Coral Pink Sand Dunes Campground → Bryce Canyon → Kodachrome Basin → Grosvenor Arch → Cottonwood Canyon Road → Stateline Campground (source: Directions from Moenave Dinosaur Tracks to Stateline Campground.kmz).

## 3. Stateline Campground → Goblin Valley (segment 3)

Stateline → Paw Hole (South Coyote Buttes) → Alstrom Point → Smokey Mountain Road → Escalante Petrified Forest SP → Upper Muley Twist Canyon → Long Dong Silver → Mars Desert Research Station (MDRS) → Goblin Valley (source: Directions from Stateline Campground to Goblin Valley.kmz).

## 4. Goblin Valley → Mt. Pennell Lookout (segment 4)

Goblin Valley → Moonscape Overlook → Cathedral Valley → Cathedral Valley Campground → Hartnet/Cathedral Road → Bentonite Hills → McMillan Spring Road → Mount Pennell / Mt. Pennell Lookout, Henry Mountains (source: Directions from Goblin Valley to Mt. Pennell Lookout.kmz).

## 5. Hanksville (BLM 0095) → Muley Point East (segment 5)

Hanksville → Shafer Trail Viewpoint → White Crack (White Rim, permit) → Mineral Bottom Switchbacks → Horse Mountain → Natural Bridges NM → Muley Point East (source: Directions from BLM 0095 Hanksville to Muley Point East.kmz).

K tomuto úseku se váže [[moab-reserve-list]] (rezervní zastávky kolem Moabu).

Pozn.: Pro segment 6 (Muley Point → Sedona → Phoenix) KMZ trasa v raw/ není — viz [[segment-6-revised-plan]].
