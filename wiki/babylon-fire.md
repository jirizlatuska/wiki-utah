# Babylon Fire (2026)

**Summary**: Wildfire burning south of the Needles district of Canyonlands as of early July 2026, with an area closure currently in effect until end of July. It forces a reroute of the Aug 11–13 days: skip the Elk Ridge / Bears Ears backcountry crossing and use highways US-191 → UT-95 → UT-261 instead.

**Sources**: User update (2026-07-05). Fire perimeter, closure boundary, and reopening date need verification against InciWeb / BLM Monticello Field Office closer to the trip.

## Situation

- Fire is located **south of the Needles district** of Canyonlands (source: user update 2026-07-05)

## Reroute (Aug 12)

After [[newspaper-rock]], return east to US-191 and go south through Monticello and Blanding; west on UT-95; optional side trip UT-275 to [[natural-bridges-nm]]; then **UT-261 south across Cedar Mesa** to the [[muley-point]] turnoff at the top of the Moki Dugway. From there, proceed as previously planned (Moki Dugway descent, [[valley-of-the-gods]], [[monument-valley]]). Newspaper Rock → Muley Point via this routing is roughly 105 miles / 2–2.5 hours (needs verification).
